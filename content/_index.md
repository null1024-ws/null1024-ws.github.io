# About Me

I interned at [Sectrend](https://www.sectrend.com.cn/) on AI program analysis with Dr. [Dizhi Zhou](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dizhizhou). Before that I was a research engineer at [Singapore Management University](https://www.smu.edu.sg/), where I was fortunate to work with Prof. [Yue Duan](https://yueduan.github.io/). In 2024, I completed my B.Eng. in Information Security at [Northwestern Polytechnical University](https://en.nwpu.edu.cn/).
